Synthesis, Jersey City

More than
a game.

Good games. Good people. Room for everyone.

Synthesis is an intermediate-level grass volleyball community in Jersey City. We play, get better, and meet new friends in a welcoming space.

Behind the games are the details: a recognizable identity, clear communication, group coordination, equipment, and a culture worth protecting. Come early, bring water, and expect to rotate.
