The route
so far.

Different roles, one habit: show up, learn the room, and make it work better for the people in it.

Jan 2023 – now
Jersey City

Grass Volleyball Coordinator

Lincoln Park Grass Volleyball

I run the weekly games: who's coming, where we play, what gear we need, and how a first-timer feels when they walk up to a group of strangers. It's part logistics and part hospitality.

The work is communication, scheduling, and keeping a community's culture welcoming as it grows. It taught me more about leading people than any title has.

May 2025 – Aug 2026
Daegu, South Korea

English Teacher

Chungdahm Institute

I moved to Daegu to teach English, and spent over a year learning how to explain things clearly in a second environment, to students who needed different things from me every day.

Teaching sharpened my patience and my ability to break a hard idea into small steps. Away from the classroom, volleyball helped me find community in a new city.

Jan 2024 – May 2025
New Jersey

Physical Therapy Aide

Ivy Rehab Network

I supported therapists and patients in a busy outpatient clinic, keeping sessions running smoothly and helping people through recovery one appointment at a time.

It was a hands-on, people-first role, and it grew out of my own interest in sport, movement, and how people rebuild.

Aug 2021 – Aug 2023
Technology

Implementation Engineer

Retarus

I worked with customers to take technical solutions from plan to launch, which meant translating between what a client needs and what the system can do.

It's where I learned to work with deadlines, documentation, and people who just want the thing to work. That mix of technology and people is still what I look for.
